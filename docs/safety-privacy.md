# GarbhaSaathi - Safety & Privacy Guidelines

## 1. Safety Guardrails & Non-Diagnostic Principles
1. **No Medical Diagnosis:** GarbhaSaathi explicitly does NOT diagnose conditions such as maternal anaemia, gestational diabetes, pre-eclampsia, intrauterine growth restriction, or clinical depression.
2. **No Prescription Changes:** The app never prescribes medications or changes supplement dosages. All reminders reflect exact clinician instructions confirmed by the woman.
3. **Emergency Warning Signs (खतरे के लक्षण):**
   - Immediate display of emergency contact guidance for:
     - Vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage
     - Severe headache with blurred vision or spots before eyes
     - Sudden severe swelling of face, hands, or legs
     - High fever with chills
     - Noticeably decreased or absent fetal movement (in 2nd/3rd trimester)
     - Persistent severe vomiting preventing hydration
   - When any danger sign is selected, the app immediately shows:
     - Urgent recommendation: **"Seek emergency medical evaluation at your nearest health centre or hospital immediately."**
     - Quick buttons to dial **108 (National Ambulance)** and **102 (Free Maternal Ambulance)**.
4. **Tone & Compassion:** No guilt-inducing streak trackers or shaming messages if meals are missed. If nausea or lack of food prevents eating, the app empathetically logs the barrier and suggests manageable hydration and rest.

## 2. Privacy & Data Integrity
1. **User Ownership:** All health data, personal profile information, and uploaded reports remain under the sole control of the user.
2. **Private Document Storage:** Reports are stored securely in local browser storage or private backend buckets with user-isolated access controls.
3. **No Third-Party Ad Trackers:** Zero behavioral tracking, advertising beacons, or data monetization.
4. **Full Data Portability & Deletion:** The user can export their entire timeline as JSON or clear all data in one click via the Privacy & Settings screen.
